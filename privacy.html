Privacy

In short: this site has no accounts, asks for no personal details, and doesn't track you.

What this site collects

Nothing. Brain Break Lab doesn't ask for your name, email or any other details. It has no sign-up, no comments and no messages, and it doesn't use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

What is saved on your device

To show the Recently opened row on the Home page, your browser remembers the last few activities you opened. This list stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can clear it with the Clear button next to the row, or by clearing your browser's site data. If your browser blocks storage, the site still works; it just won't remember.

The activities

Each activity is a separate project, hosted on GitHub Pages and shown inside this site in a frame. When you open one, your browser connects to GitHub Pages to load it, as it would for any website. Activities may save your progress (such as best scores or unlocked levels) on your own device.
